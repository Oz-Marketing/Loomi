A team is a named group of users. Teams exist so work can be assigned to a
function rather than a person — the design team, the media team — and so
assignment survives somebody leaving.

# Where teams show up

- **Projects.** Tasks and initiatives can belong to a team, and boards can be
  filtered by one.
- **Pacing.** Ads carry the people responsible for them, and alerts route to
  those people.

# Keeping them useful

Teams are only worth having if they are current. A team with three people who
left is worse than no team, because work assigned to it silently reaches nobody.

Review membership when someone joins or leaves. It takes a minute and it is the
difference between a routing mechanism and a decoration.
