Loomi is the platform Oz Marketing runs client marketing on. Audiences, email
and text campaigns, automation, forms and landing pages, ad creative, paid-media
budgets, and client reporting all live in one place, under one set of accounts.

That matters more than it sounds. Because a contact, a send, an ad dollar, and a
report all sit in the same database, Loomi can answer questions that used to
require exporting from three tools and reconciling them by hand — which
campaign produced the leads, what an acquisition actually cost, whether a
budget landed where it was planned.

# The four places you can be

Loomi is one product, but it opens on four different addresses depending on what
you are doing. The switcher at the bottom of the sidebar moves you between the
ones you have access to.

| Where | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Studio** | The production workspace. Audiences, campaigns, templates, flows, forms, landing pages, ad creative, assets. |
| **Reporting** | The client-facing dashboards. Ads, websites, local presence, sales and service, budget. |
| **Projects** | Internal delivery — initiatives, tasks, calendars — plus the ad planning and pacing tools. |
| **Agency settings** | Platform configuration. Opens as a panel over whatever you were doing, from the cog in the top bar. |

You will not necessarily see all four. What you can open depends on the roles
you hold — see [Roles and access](/docs/roles-and-access).

# Everything is scoped to an account

Almost every screen in Loomi is showing you one account's data. The account
picker at the top of the sidebar is therefore the most important control on the
page: change it and the contacts, campaigns, reports, and creative all change
with it.

Some accounts are **groups** — a dealer group, for example, that owns several
rooftops. Standing in a group gives you the option to roll its children up into
one view. [Accounts and scope](/docs/accounts-and-scope) covers how that works
and where it does and doesn't apply.

# What is native and what is not

Loomi is not a skin over someone else's tool. Contacts, templates, campaigns,
sends, engagement, ad spend, and analytics are all Loomi's own records.

A few outside services do the parts that genuinely have to happen outside:
delivering email, delivering text messages, and reading spend back from the ad
platforms. Those are pipes. The data and the logic are here, which is why the
channels can be stitched together at all.

:::note
Loomi is built to work for any local business, not just car dealers. Automotive
and powersports are the most developed because that is where the work has been,
but nothing in the platform assumes it. Where you see industry-specific
behavior, it is driven by the Industry setting on the account.
:::
