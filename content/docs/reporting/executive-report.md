The executive report pulls the headline figures across every channel into one
page — the version for someone who has ten minutes and a decision to make.

# Preparing it

1. **Set the date range to a whole period.** A month-to-date against a full
   previous month reads as a decline that isn't there.
2. **Read every number yourself first.** Anything you cannot explain in a
   sentence is either a data problem or the most interesting thing on the page.
   Both need finding before the meeting, not during it.
3. **Pick the one thing.** A page of numbers with no argument is a page nobody
   acts on.

# What it is not

It is a summary, not an analysis. It says what happened, not why. The
explanation is yours — the underlying reports are where you find it.

:::tip
Where the account has an ad meeting view, it is built for the same conversation
at a media level: what ran, what it cost, what it produced, what is proposed
next. Use it alongside this rather than instead of it.
:::
