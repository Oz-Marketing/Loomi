Site and landing page activity over the period: visits, where they came from,
what people did on the page, and how many converted.

# Reading a landing page

Three numbers in order, because each one narrows where the problem is:

1. **Views.** Low? The problem is upstream — the ad, the email, the audience.
2. **Scroll depth and time.** People arriving and leaving immediately means the
   page isn't delivering what brought them.
3. **Submissions.** Good engagement and no submissions points at the form: too
   long, asking for too much, or broken.

A page converting at a low rate with strong engagement is a form problem far
more often than a copy problem.

# Sources

Where visits came from — a campaign, an ad, a direct visit. Traffic driven from
a Loomi campaign is tagged automatically, which is what makes "did that email
actually send anyone" answerable.

# What this doesn't cover

This reports on pages Loomi publishes. The client's main website, unless it is
built here, is measured by whatever analytics that site uses.
