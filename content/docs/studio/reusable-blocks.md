A reusable block is a chunk of content — a hours-and-address panel, a
disclaimer, a promotional banner, a standard footer — stored once and dropped
into as many pages as you like.

Change the block, and every page using it changes.

# When to make one

Make a block when the same content appears in more than two places **and** would
have to be corrected in all of them if it changed.

The address is the standard example. Store hours are better. A one-off headline
is not — a block used once is an extra place to look for no benefit.

# What it saves you

The value is not the typing. It is that when the phone number changes, you
change it once instead of finding eleven pages, missing two, and discovering
them six months later.

:::tip
Name blocks for what they contain, not where they are used. `Store hours`
survives a redesign; `Homepage bottom section` doesn't.
:::
